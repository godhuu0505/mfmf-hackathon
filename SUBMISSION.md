## Purpose and vision

Care information is often scattered between a family and the people who look after a pet. mfmf brings daily notes, photos, weight, care schedules and permissioned sharing into one place. The goal is a continuity of care in which the pet, family and care providers can understand what happened and decide what needs attention.

Health, safe choices, memories and a sustainable connection to clinics, salons and rescue communities are the wider vision. Marketplace, medical integrations and 3D/AR ideas must be described as future plans unless they have actually been implemented and verified.
